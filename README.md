# Northstar Retail Sales & Customer Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

Northstar Retail Ltd. is a UK-based general retailer seeking to better understand its sales performance, profitability, customer behaviour and regional performance.

This project uses Excel to analyse two years of retail transaction data covering 2024–2025 and develops an interactive management dashboard to support business decision-making.

## Business Questions

The analysis addresses key management questions including:

* How have sales and profit changed over time?
* Which customer types generate the most revenue?
* Which product categories are most profitable?
* Which regions are performing strongest and weakest?
* Which products generate the highest revenue and profit?
* Who are the highest-value customers?
* How does average order value vary across regions, customer types and categories?
* How has the customer base changed between 2024 and 2025?

## Key Insights

### 1. Strong overall profitability

Northstar generated approximately **£1.38 million in sales** and **£579 thousand in profit** across the two-year period, resulting in an overall profit margin of approximately **42%**.

### 2. Electronics generated the highest profit

Although Home & Kitchen generated the highest revenue among the major categories, Electronics produced the highest total profit, highlighting the importance of analysing profitability alongside revenue.

### 3. Customer acquisition declined significantly

New-customer activity declined substantially between 2024 and 2025, while returning-customer activity increased.

This suggests that Northstar should investigate customer acquisition and retention strategies, including promotional activity and marketing effectiveness.

### 4. Regional performance varies significantly

The analysis identified substantial differences in sales, profitability and customer activity across regions.

In particular, North East and Northern Ireland require further investigation because of their relatively weak profitability performance.

### 5. High-value customers contribute disproportionately

The top customers generate substantial sales and profit, highlighting opportunities for targeted retention and customer relationship strategies.
